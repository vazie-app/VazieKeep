## What a fork or redistributed build must do

A build you distribute — in an app store, on a website or anywhere else — that is not the official one published from
[github.com/vazie-app/VazieKeep](https://github.com/vazie-app/VazieKeep):

- uses its own name, not "Vazie", "Vazie Keep" or "Keep+", nor a confusingly similar one;
- replaces the app icons and the store graphics with its own;
- uses its own application ID (not `app.vazie.keep`) and its own signing key;
- does not point at Vazie's servers or website unless Vazovsky
  agrees, and does not offer, sell or unlock Keep+;
